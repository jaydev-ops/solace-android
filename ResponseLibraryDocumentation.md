# Solace+ — Response Library & Therapeutic Content Specification

---

## 1. Response Design Principles & Proactive Conversational Behavior

Every template in the Solace+ response library is human-authored to meet strict psychological safety, emotional validation, and non-judgmental accompaniment standards.

### 1.1 Proactive Emotional Support Architecture
Following clinical and conversational refinement, the assistant does **not** make users repeatedly ask for basic emotional care through permission-seeking barriers (e.g. *"Would you like me to suggest something?"*, *"Do you want to try a breathing exercise?"*, *"Would you like to talk about it?"*). 

Instead, every response follows a natural, proactive conversational cadence:
1. **Emotional Acknowledgment**: Explicitly recognizes the user's current feeling without minimizing it.
2. **Validation**: Affirms that the feeling makes sense given human vulnerability, stress, and nervous system biology.
3. **Direct Supportive Guidance**: Directly and gently introduces an appropriate, evidence-based coping strategy (e.g., jaw unclench & slow exhale for anxiety, sensory 5-4-3-2-1 grounding for overthinking, breaking tasks down for academic stress, gentle human connection for loneliness, taking physical space for anger).
4. **Gentle Continuation**: Leaves an open, unpressured door for continued exploration without demanding a response.

### 1.2 Non-Instructional Sensitive Moments (Grief & Loss)
For categories where active instruction is clinically counterproductive (such as grief, acute bereavement, or traumatic heartbreak), the system does **not** force exercises or breathing homework. It provides **compassionate, steady presence**, validating the pain and honoring the silence.

### 1.3 Strict Anti-Toxic Positivity Invariants
The response library strictly forbids dismissive, invalidating, or falsely cheerful tropes:
- ❌ *"Everything happens for a reason."*
- ❌ *"Just be positive."*
- ❌ *"Others have it worse."*
- ❌ *"Stop overthinking."*
- ❌ *"Everything will be fine."*
- ❌ *"You need to be stronger."*
- ❌ *"Cheer up."*

## 4. Crisis Safety Separate Response Pool

Crisis responses operate in their own clinical safety pool, strictly partitioned from casual responses:
- **Level 1 (Distress)**: Validates severe emotional distress, introduces gentle holding space, and checks for physical safety.
- **Level 2 (Passive Ideation / Burdensomeness)**: Inquires calmly and directly about suicidal ideation (`CURRENT_SUICIDAL_THOUGHTS`).
- **Level 3 (Active Ideation)**: Immediately interrupts casual conversation, evaluates intent/timeframe/means, and introduces verified 24/7 helplines.
- **Level 4 (Imminent Emergency / Danger)**: Direct emergency intervention displaying click-to-call **112** (India) / **988** (US/Canada) and Tele-MANAS (**14416**).
