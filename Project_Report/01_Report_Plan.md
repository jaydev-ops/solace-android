## 1. Document Architecture & Pagination Strategy

The academic report strictly conforms to the 54-page benchmark demonstrated in the university reference PDF. It employs conventional two-tier academic numbering:
- **Preliminary Pages:** Lowercase Roman numerals (`i` to `v`), beginning from the Abstract.
- **Main Body Chapters & References:** Arabic numerals (`1` to `45+`), beginning from Chapter 1 Page 1 through Chapter 9 and References.

## 3. Typographical and Stylistic Standards

- **Paper Size:** Standard ISO A4 Portrait (`210 mm x 297 mm`).
- **Body Font:** Times New Roman / Serif, 12 pt size, fully justified text, 1.5 line spacing.
- **Chapter Headings:** Bold, 16 pt, centred, capitalized, prefixed with "Chapter N".
- **Major Section Headings (Level 1):** Bold, 14 pt, left-aligned, numbered (e.g., `1.1`, `4.2`).
- **Subsection Headings (Level 2):** Bold, 12 pt, left-aligned, numbered (e.g., `1.2.1`, `4.3.2`).
- **Captions:**
  - Figure captions: Centred beneath the illustration (e.g., `Figure 4.2.1: Proposed System Workflow`).
  - Table titles: Left-aligned or centred directly above the table (e.g., `Table 1.2.1: Literature review analysis`).
- **Page Margins:** Left: 1.25 inches (binding gutter), Right: 0.75 inches, Top: 1.0 inch, Bottom: 1.0 inch.
