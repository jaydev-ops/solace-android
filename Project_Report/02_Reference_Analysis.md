## 1. Visual & Formatting Benchmark Observations

Thorough visual inspection and OCR analysis of all 54 pages of `Mini Project report.pdf` reveals the following concrete layout principles:

1. **Title Page Layout (Page 1):**
   - Centered double-quoted project title at top: `“Project Name”` (approx. 18–20 pt Bold).
   - Degree submission text:
     ```
     Submitted in partial fulfilment of the requirements
     for the degree of
     Bachelor of Technology
     in
     CSE AIML Engineering
     ```
   - Attribution: `by / Student Names (Roll No.)`.
   - Guide details: `Guide / Asst. Prof. Swapna Ambekar`.
   - College / University Emblem centered in the middle.
   - Department and College block:
     ```
     Department of CSE AIML Engineering
     Shri Bhagubhai Mafatlal Polytechnic And College of Engineering
     (UG Programmes Affiliated to Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Technological University, Lonere)
     (Ac. Yr. 2026-27)
     ```

2. **Certificates & Formal Preliminary Pages (Pages 2 to 5):**
   - **Page 2:** `CERTIFICATE` with signatory lines for Mini Project Guide, Third Year Co-Ordinator, and Principal.
   - **Page 3:** `CERTIFICATE OF APPROVAL OF EXAMINERS` specifying Semester V and Ac. Yr. 2026-2027, with signatories for External Examiner and Internal Examiner.
   - **Page 4:** `DECLARATION` of academic honesty and anti-plagiarism commitment, with candidate names, signatures, Date, and Place.
   - **Page 5:** `ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS` expressing institutional and faculty gratitude.

3. **Academic Indexing & Metadata (Pages 6 to 10):**
   - **Page 6:** `ABSTRACT` (page numbered with Roman numeral `i`).
   - **Page 7:** `CONTENTS` table with Chapter and subsection mappings (Roman numeral `ii`).
   - **Page 8:** `LIST OF FIGURES` table with Figure No., Title, and Page No. (Roman numeral `iii`).
   - **Page 9:** `LIST OF TABLES` table with Table No., Title, and Page No. (Roman numeral `iv`).
   - **Page 10:** `ABBREVIATIONS AND ACRONYMS` table defining standard technical terms (Roman numeral `v`).

4. **Main Body Chapter Architecture (Pages 11 to 50):**
   - Each Chapter starts on a fresh page with a bold centered title:
     ```
     Chapter N
     [Chapter Title]
     ```
   - Subsections are left-aligned and numbered (e.g., `1.1 Introduction & Motivation`, `1.2 Literature Review`, `4.1 Software Requirements`, `4.2 Workflow`).
   - Literature Review contains a detailed comparative table (`Table 1.2.1: Literature review analysis`) with columns: `Sr.No.`, `Author`, `Technique`, `Dataset`, `Algorithm`, `Application`.
   - Methodology contains a clear workflow schematic (`Figure 4.2.1 proposed system`) with input, processing, model execution, and outputs.
   - Cost analysis provides hardware, software, operational, and maintenance costs.
   - Testing outlines methodology, input samples, UI screenshots, and validation steps.
   - Results & Discussion presents comprehensive tables and visual performance charts.
   - Summary (Chapter 8) and Conclusion (Chapter 9) synthesize technical outcomes and future work.

5. **References Section (Pages 51 to 52):**
   - Titled `REFERENCES` in bold centered font.
   - Standard IEEE numbered citation format `[1]` through `[15]` with author names, paper titles, publication sources, dates, and DOIs/URLs.
